## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished macOS app icon for a developer utility called Codex Auth, used at 48x48 in native notification banners. A compact retro-futuristic robot head, confident and friendly, in a clean pixel-inspired geometric style. Large ivory-white helmet silhouette with a deep teal face visor and two simple luminous cyan eyes. Subtle stepped pixel corners and one small coral-orange signal light on the helmet; a simple curved refresh signal integrated into the silhouette if it remains extremely clear. The robot should occupy most of the icon with a distinctive readable silhouette, straight front view. Rich deep teal rounded-square tile, restrained cyan highlights and warm ivory surfaces, premium editorial feel with very subtle depth. Minimal composition: no letters, no typography, no numbers, no badge, no tiny detail, no busy halftone texture, no terminal prompt, no OpenAI knot logo, no human/anime face. Transparent canvas outside the rounded-square tile, generous even 8% transparent outer margin; perfectly centered. Square 1024x1024 production asset, crisp shape edges suitable for resizing. Deliver only the final single icon, not a contact sheet or mockup.
